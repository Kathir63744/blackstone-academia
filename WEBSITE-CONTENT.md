## 3. About — who it's for

Super Admin · Admin & Staff · Academic Coach · Supervisor · Teacher · Student/Parent
Key differentiators: custom roles & permissions (RBAC), package-based student rights, sibling accounts with combined/separate invoicing, auto teacher replacement on leave.

## 4. Features (8 cards)
1. **Student portal** — trials, session tracking, curriculum (PDF/PPT/video), assignments, rankings, one-tap class join
2. **Teacher & coach portals** — auto IDs, schedules, earnings, leave + auto-replacement, trial oversight
3. **Live classes & recording** — Zoom / Teams / Meet / Jitsi; auto-upload recordings to SharePoint / OneDrive / Drive
4. **Billing & finance** — Stripe, PayPal, auto-invoices, multi-currency (USD/GBP/AED), Wise expense tracking
5. **Assessments & rankings** — instant results, auto-grading, survey-based rankings
6. **Communication** — real-time chat (files, audio), email templates, WhatsApp & SMS alerts
7. **Analytics & reports** — trial funnels, attendance/drop-off, payment status, automated daily/weekly/monthly reports
8. **Security** — SSO (Google/Apple), 2FA/MFA, single-session limit, country restrictions, auto-deactivation

## 5. Customization (interactive tabs)
- **Roles & permissions:** unlimited custom roles, per-module access, role-specific dashboards
- **Student packages:** Simple / Essential / Elite / Pro; per-package reschedule & cancellation rights
- **Branding & workflows:** logo, colors, domain, custom email/invoice templates, notification rules
